CarCast Mirror Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be completed by the publisher · Operator/contact: to be completed by the publisher

Short version: CarCast Mirror sends user-requested screen and eligible playback audio directly to an approved local receiver. It does not upload casting media to a CarCast cloud service.

What CarCast handles

When you start a casting session, CarCast handles your device display and, only when you enable the option and grant Android permission, eligible playback audio. It also handles temporary receiver names, browser user-agent text, local network endpoints, connection state, and technical diagnostics.

How casting data is used

This information is used to discover a nearby receiver, authenticate the intended peer, establish an encrypted session, stream the content you requested, adapt quality, and troubleshoot failures. CarCast does not use screen frames or audio samples to select advertisements.

Where casting data goes

Screen, audio, signaling, and receiver metadata are sent directly between the phone and the receiver you select or approve over the local network. Native signaling uses TLS and WebRTC media uses DTLS-SRTP. CarCast does not operate a cloud relay for this content.

Advertising and consent

Beta builds may show a small Google Mobile Ads banner only on an idle device-selection surface. Ads are disabled during casting, receiver mode, connection establishment, receiver approval, MediaProjection consent, permission flows, and automotive interaction. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform process information according to Google's policies and the consent choices available in your region. CarCast does not send screen, audio, WebRTC media, receiver debug data, or SAS values to those SDKs.

Storage and deletion

CarCast does not intentionally record or store media. Limited technical diagnostics may remain in app-private storage until you use the Diagnostics CLEAR action or clear app data. Uninstalling the app removes app-private data. Advertising SDK cache and consent records are managed by their respective SDKs and Android app storage.

Permissions

Android MediaProjection is requested for each screen-share session. RECORD_AUDIO is requested only for the optional device-playback audio path; protected or private audio may be unavailable. Network permissions support direct local discovery and transport. Notifications support active foreground-service status and Stop actions.

Children, changes, and contact

CarCast is not designed as a child-directed service. The publisher will complete the operator contact details and update this policy before public distribution. Privacy questions should be sent to the publisher contact shown above.
