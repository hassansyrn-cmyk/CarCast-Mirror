CarCast Mirror Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026
Publisher and operator: Hasan Hussein
Privacy contact: hassan.syrn@gmail.com

In brief: CarCast Mirror sends screen content and, when you choose to enable it, eligible device playback audio directly to a receiver that you select or approve on your local network. CarCast does not operate a cloud relay or upload your casting media to a CarCast server. The beta may display a banner ad while the device is idle; Google's advertising SDK handles ad-related data as described below.

Information used for casting

When you start a session, the app accesses the screen content you choose to mirror through Android's MediaProjection consent flow. If you enable optional audio sharing and grant the required Android permission, it can capture eligible device playback audio using Android playback-capture features; this is not intended to record microphone conversations. Protected or private audio may not be available.

To discover a receiver, establish and maintain a connection, and troubleshoot it, the app may handle temporary receiver names, browser user-agent text, local-network addresses or endpoints, connection state, and technical session diagnostics. These details are used for the casting session and troubleshooting. The app does not send screen frames, playback audio, WebRTC media, receiver debug data, or session-verification values to its advertising or consent SDKs.

Where casting information goes

Casting media and connection signaling travel directly between your phone and the receiver you select or approve over the local network. CarCast does not run a cloud relay for this content. Native signaling uses encrypted TLS transport, and WebRTC media uses DTLS-SRTP encryption. Only mirror to a receiver you trust.

Advertising and consent services

Release builds may show a small Google Mobile Ads banner on an idle device-selection screen. Ads are disabled during active casting and other protected setup or receiver flows. CarCast does not operate a separate analytics service.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google an IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views, diagnostics such as app or SDK launch time, hang rate, and energy use, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID (and signed-in account identifiers where applicable). Google describes these data uses as supporting advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and states that SDK-collected data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google User Messaging Platform may present and process privacy or consent choices where applicable. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, Google's ads privacy guidance, and Google's Privacy Policy for details and controls.

CarCast does not request precise device location. The approximate location that Google may infer from an advertising request's IP address is handled by Google's SDK and services, not used by CarCast to locate you for casting.

Storage, retention, and deletion

CarCast does not intentionally record or store mirrored screen media or playback audio. Limited technical diagnostics may remain in app-private storage on your device until you use the Diagnostics CLEAR action, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The app does not maintain a CarCast user account or server database of casting sessions. Google SDK data and consent records are handled under Google's retention practices and controls.

Permissions

  • Android MediaProjection asks for your approval for each screen-sharing session.
  • The optional playback-audio path requires the relevant Android permission and is subject to Android's capture restrictions.
  • Internet and network-state access support local receiver discovery and direct transport.
  • Notifications show active foreground-service status and provide controls such as stopping a session.

Children

CarCast Mirror is a general-purpose mirroring utility and is not designed as a child-directed service. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a CarCast account or CarCast-operated server. Advertising SDK practices are described above and are subject to the consent and controls available in the user's region.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop a casting session at any time and clear locally stored diagnostics as described above. For questions about this policy, or a privacy request concerning information handled by the app, contact hassan.syrn@gmail.com. Google advertising data is handled by Google; consult Google's linked privacy and advertising resources for its controls and requests.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published.
